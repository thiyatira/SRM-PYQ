Team: Blaze
Project: SRM StudyHub
Track: Campus Innovation & Student Life

PROBLEM STATEMENT
SRM students revise from previous year papers scattered across WhatsApp groups and blurry photos, which are hard to search, hard to read and give no sense of which topics actually repeat.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION
SRM StudyHub is a mobile-first web app that puts all SRM previous year questions in one searchable place. Questions are organised by course code, and students can filter them by unit and marks. Math and code display clearly, and students can bookmark questions and share direct links to them, with no login needed. We are adding a practice mode built from real papers, a topic heatmap that shows which units repeat, and an AI assistant that explains answers and cites the paper each question came from.
